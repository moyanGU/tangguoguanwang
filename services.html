ULINK TECH / EST. 2022

Veteran craft, organized for the next content market.

Founded in 2022, Ulink Tech is an integrated content creation and global distribution company. Its core management team brings more than 30 years of experience in film and animation, with production involvement in theatrical features and leading animation IP including Kung Fu Panda 2 and Soul Land. That background gives the studio both an industrial production discipline and a high creative standard.

Ulink Tech is now focused on AI comic drama alongside premium live-action short drama. Its flagship Moge's Extraordinary Highway series has exceeded 1.2 billion official-content plays across the web. The company continues to combine traditional film-production experience with AI tools to grow its content slate and global distribution reach.
