Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ulink Tech, through its Candy Studio content label, handles information when you use CandyStudio Drama inside TikTok or through an authorized preview environment.

1. Information we process

  • TikTok account identifier: an app-specific OpenID and authorization credentials supplied through TikTok's secure login flow.
  • Service activity: titles opened, episode position, feature interactions, entitlement status, and related timestamps when these features are enabled.
  • Technical information: device or browser type, app language, network and error logs, IP address, and security signals reasonably needed to operate the service.
  • Support information: information you choose to include when contacting us.

We do not request your TikTok password. We do not request your email address, phone number, profile name, or avatar through TikTok unless a future feature clearly asks for separate permission.

2. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate sessions, deliver video and episode features, remember progress, support authorized purchases when enabled, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose errors, respond to requests, meet legal obligations, and improve service reliability.

3. Cookies and sessions

We use a strictly necessary, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in to CandyStudio Drama. It is not used for cross-site advertising. TikTok may separately process information under its own privacy policy.

4. Sharing

We may share only the information needed with TikTok, hosting and security providers, content-delivery providers, professional advisers, or authorities when required by law. We do not sell personal information. Providers may process information only for contracted services and appropriate safeguards apply.

5. International processing

Because CandyStudio Drama serves international audiences, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use contractual, organizational, or other lawful safeguards for international transfers.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including account continuity, legal compliance, dispute handling, and security. Authorization credentials are kept on the server and are not exposed to the browser. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and may have the right to appeal or complain to a regulator. You may also revoke connected-app permissions through TikTok. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

8. Children

CandyStudio Drama is not directed to children below the minimum age required by TikTok or applicable law. If we learn that information was collected contrary to those requirements, we will take appropriate steps to delete or restrict it.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy as the service or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated date and provide additional notice when required.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and rights requests may be sent to gary.tu@ulinktech.us.
